07Frequently Asked Questions

Is Forge Control really free?

Yes. The site and all content are free, MIT licensed. No accounts, no paywalls.

Do I install anything?

No. Forge Control is a website — open it and read. Nothing runs on your PC.

Where are my devices managed?

Right here in the guide: list your rig, pick lighting and fan profiles, and apply them with your own tools.

Does it work without ASUS hardware?

Forge Control is hardware-agnostic — nothing here is tied to one vendor.

Will it slow down my PC?

No — it's a static web page. Close the tab and it uses zero resources.

Is there a desktop app?

Being built now — Electron app is next on the roadmap.

Who built this?

FORGESTUDIO-303. Hand-built UI and code, MIT licensed. Independent project — not affiliated with ASUS or ROG.
