What is Forge Control?

One dashboard for your whole rig — no juggling a dozen utilities. Lighting, cooling, monitoring, games and cloud sync in a single hand-built interface.

01

Forge Glow Lighting

Sync color and effects across every device. Static, Breathing, Strobing, Rainbow and Wave presets plus a custom color picker — applied everywhere in one click.

02

Performance Modes

Silent, Balanced, Turbo and Manual. One click retunes fans and cooling behavior for gaming, streaming or quiet work.

03

Live Monitoring

Real-time CPU, GPU, fan and temperature cards with a sparkline. Bottlenecks show up before they become problems.

04

Game Library

Every title in one place with per-game launch and optimize actions. Profiles follow your games, not the other way round.

05

Cloud Sync

Sign in with Google or GitHub and your devices, lighting and fan setups sync across machines from Neon Postgres.

06

Fan Curves

Per-fan sliders with a live curve canvas. Quiet when idle, aggressive under load — tuned by you in seconds.

Getting Started

From zero to tuned rig in five minutes.

1

Open the app

No installer — it runs right here on this page. Scroll to Your Rig, Live and everything is already loaded.

2

Sign in to sync

Hit Google or GitHub login up top. Your setup syncs to the cloud so it follows you between machines.

3

Check your devices

Your device cards load from the cloud automatically. Hit Configure on any card to manage it.

4

Set your glow

Pick a color and effect in Forge Glow, then Apply to all. Watch the preview strip — that's exactly what your rig gets.

5

Tune your fans

Start with Balanced. Gaming hard? Switch to Turbo. Working late? Silent. The curve canvas shows the result live.

6

Build your library

Hit Optimize on a game card for a tuned profile, Launch when you're ready to play.

7

Back it up

Download your config JSON from the Download section. Re-import anytime — your rig, your rules.

Real Questions. Real Answers.

Is Forge Control really free?

Yes. The web app, cloud sync and all features are free. Sign-in is only needed for syncing — browsing and tuning locally costs nothing.

Do I install anything?

No. Forge Control runs entirely in your browser. Your config downloads as a small JSON file you can keep or re-import.

How does cloud sync work?

Sign in with Google or GitHub. Devices, lighting and fan setups save to Neon Postgres and load on any machine you sign into.

My devices aren't showing. What now?

Check the status pill up top. "Connecting…" means the API is unreachable — check your connection and refresh. Signed out? Sign back in to reload your cloud devices.

Does it work without ASUS hardware?

Forge Control is hardware-agnostic — it manages profiles, lighting presets, fan curves and your library for any rig. Nothing here is tied to one vendor.

Will it slow down my PC?

No — it's a web page, not a background service. Close the tab and it uses zero resources. No drivers, no services, no tray apps.

Is there a desktop app?

Not yet — that's next on the roadmap (Electron/Tauri). The web app is the full experience today.

Who built this?

FORGESTUDIO-303. Hand-built UI and code, MIT licensed. Independent project — not affiliated with ASUS or ROG.
